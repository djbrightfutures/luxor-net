# Luxor Net Technologies

**New, proven, easy-to-use security methods — including security that AI systems can safely implement.**

Founded by **Christian Rivera (DJ BrightFuture)**. Open source.

Many teams don't let their AI touch real security work. Luxor Net's aim is to change that:
publish security methods that are **simple to adopt** and **proven in the open**, so that
developers — and the AI assistants building alongside them — can implement strong protection
without inventing risky crypto from scratch. Our goal is to earn the trust of individuals and
large organizations by showing the work: open code, honest limits, real tests.

## Inventions

### 🔑 [Luxor Net Key](./luxor-net-key/) — *a secret picture is your key*
Derive an encryption key from the **exact bytes of a secret picture** (best: a photo of your
own random pencil drawing). Zero-knowledge: the picture is never stored, only ciphertext is.
A wrong picture can't open your data. → **[Live demo](./luxor-net-key/demo.html)** ·
**[Docs & security notes](./luxor-net-key/README.md)**

## Principles

- **Prove it, then publish it.** Every method ships with tests that show it does what it claims.
- **State the limits.** A security tool that hides its weaknesses is lying. Ours are written down plainly.
- **Zero-knowledge by default.** Your keys and secrets never leave your device.
- **Easy to use.** A method no one can adopt protects no one.
