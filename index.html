Many teams don't let their AI touch real security work. Luxor Net's aim is to change that: publish security methods that are simple to adopt and proven in the open, so developers — and the AI assistants building alongside them — can implement strong protection without inventing risky crypto from scratch. Our goal is to earn the trust of individuals and large organizations by showing the work: open code, honest limits, real tests.

Principles

  • Prove it, then publish it. Every method ships with tests that show it does what it claims.
  • State the limits. A security tool that hides its weaknesses is lying. Ours are written plainly.
  • Zero-knowledge by default. Your keys and secrets never leave your device.
  • Easy to use. A method no one can adopt protects no one.
